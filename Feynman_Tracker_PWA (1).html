The Feynman Technique

1. Choose a concept — Pick a topic from your UPSC syllabus.

2. Teach it to a child — Explain it in the simplest terms. If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.

3. Identify gaps — Go back to the source material when you get stuck or can't explain something.

4. Simplify and review — Refine your explanation. Use analogies and simplify further.

5. Rate yourself — Give yourself an honest Feynman Score (1-10) on how well you could teach the concept.
